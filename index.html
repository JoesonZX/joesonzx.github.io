ABOUT ME

I recently finished my B.A. in Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Minnesota (May 2026, GPA 3.83/4.0) and was elected to Phi Beta Kappa. My focus is on LLM-powered multi-agent systems — making agents that don't just chat, but perceive, reason about, and act on real infrastructure.

Since May 2025 I've been an undergraduate research assistant with Prof. Zhi-Li Zhang at UMN NetLab, co-designing A²RAN, a multi-agent framework that lets wireless operators run radio access networks in natural language. Before that I led a 4-person team on prostate-MRI computer vision, benchmarked ResNet-50 vs. ViT in a published paper, and spent a summer as a penetration-testing intern (300+ targets, 10+ confirmed vulnerabilities).
